Dance2Music for Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later. Google Chrome is required
and installed separately.

Open the DMG, drag Dance2Music to Applications, then open the app. First setup
downloads and verifies approximately 4.9 GB of models and browser assets.
The app opens Chrome when ready. Subsequent use works offline.

The existing SegFormer scene model is limited to non-commercial research or
evaluation under its NVIDIA license; original notices accompany its assets.

For updates, Quit Dance2Music and replace the app in Applications. Compatible,
verified downloads are preserved. The app does not update itself.

See the [setup and troubleshooting guide](https://github.com/Music-Intelligence-Lab/dance2music/blob/main/docs/google-magenta.md).
Installer integrity is recorded in SHA256SUMS and release-manifest.json.
