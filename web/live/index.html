Help · watches missing from the chooser?

Connect watch automatically shows devices whose names start with M5StickC-IMU-. Select your watch: M5StickC-IMU-00-L/R or M5StickC-IMU-01-L/R (or your assigned pairs).

If the chooser stays empty, fully quit Chrome (⌘Q on Mac), then reopen this page and try Connect watch again. Closing a tab or window is not enough. Try this even when Chrome already has Bluetooth permission.

Wait a few seconds for names starting with M5StickC-IMU-. On a Mac, allow Google Chrome in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth, then fully quit and reopen Chrome. Disconnect watches from other apps, phones, or browser tabs before retrying.

Help · pairing & reconnecting

Chrome selects one watch at a time. After Pair, choose Connect next watch to add another. You can use fewer than four watches. Automatic connection restores approved watches when supported by the browser. Disconnect pauses that watch until Reconnect or the next page load. Samples are saved during recording.

If your browser cannot restore paired watches, select them again after reopening the page. Previously selected watches are hidden from the chooser.

Help · sphere

The sphere sits between your wrists; its radius is half their distance. Choose the Sphere mapping preset to make its volume (r³) the instrument's loudness.

Drag objects to move them. Tap with a hand or foot to play.

Turn off to put the black keys on the far edge. You can change this while playing.

Help · interacting with the scene

Use a live camera in Chrome on Apple Silicon. Stop the session before changing scenes.

Drag an object with the mouse to move it onto a detected floor or tabletop. At an edge, it settles at the closest fitting spot. Show surfaces reveals available areas. Esc cancels a drag; Rescan resets positions.

Tap a shape with a hand or foot for sparks and a note in the current chord. Adjust “objects” in Mix. Touches are approximate.
Yellow boxes are touch areas. Cyan dots have hand/foot depth; yellow dots are still waiting for depth.

Help · percussion

Bounce steadily from the knees: the groove starts once the hips have been clearly periodic for about a second, and follows your tempo. A ring at the hips marks each beat; if the drums feel early or late against your body, move Sync offset.
